Who we are

Upperhand Foundation Uganda is a children’s support organisation working in Mityana District, Uganda. We stand beside vulnerable children and their communities so that every child can learn, stay healthy and be cared for.

Our support covers education, healthcare, nutrition, clothing, hygiene and other basic needs.
