Who we are

Upperhand Foundation Uganda is a children’s support foundation serving the Mityana District community. We support vulnerable children through education, healthcare, nutrition, clothing, hygiene and other basic needs.

Our mission

To empower vulnerable children through education, healthcare and social support.

Our vision

A future where every child can thrive, regardless of background.
